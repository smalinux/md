# How to Design a Custom ESP32 PCB in 19 Minutes (Full Tutorial)

A quick guide on how to design an ESP-32 PCB using KiCad.

Please note:
As pointed out in the comments, if you&#39;re following along you&#39;re going to want to make the USB traces a differential pair. Also please take this video with a grain of salt as I really was going as fast as I could and may have missed something! Real PCB design takes much longer than 19 minutes, and it&#39;s important to take the proper care to ensure you get the intended performance!
